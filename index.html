Who We Are

25 Years of Trusted Sales & Service Across Karnataka

Sri Annapurneshwari Electricals has served customers across Karnataka since 2001. Based in Chitradurga and led by proprietor N. Lokesh, we are your trusted single-window partner for complete electrical and solar energy solutions.

Our range covers solar & heat-pump water heaters, gas & electric geysers, water softeners, RO purifiers, pressure booster pumps, solar rooftop systems and Exide power backup — with genuine products, professional installation and dependable after-sales service.
